Open Source Data Tools for DOT Contracts & Lettings

Powered by Linux & ADHD

🔍 Open PDF Search

Elasticsearch-based PDF OCR text search tool. Run keyword searches on all available historical IDOT contract letting documents.

🌉 Example Bridge Plans

Find historical IDOT bridge and box culvert plans by structure type, span, county and construction (new, rehab, maintenance). Each result opens the official IDOT plan sheet.

📊 Open Bids

Solara-based search and filter tool for IDOT contract bid data and cost estimating.

📊 Open Tolls

Solara-based search and filter tool for ISTHA contract bid data and cost estimating.

📋 Open Selections-PTB

Solara-based search and filter tool for IDOT PTB consultant selection results.

📋 Open Selections-PSB

Solara-based search and filter tool for ISTHA PSB consultant selection results.

📋 Open Selections-ODOT

Solara-based search and filter tool for Ohio DOT programmatic consultant selections, letters of interest, and firm histories.

📚 Open Bulletins

Search historical IDOT PTB and Illinois Tollway PSB bulletins, including revisions and addenda. An independent research archive—not a current procurement listing.

ℹ️ About Open DOT

Open DOT is an independent, free-to-use platform dedicated to making publicly available transportation and construction information easier to review and search.

All information presented through Open DOT is obtained directly from publicly accessible government websites, databases, and public agencies. Open DOT does not use or reference any proprietary or commercially aggregated datasets.

Our sole purpose is to organize and present public information in a more accessible and useful format, supporting independent research, transparency, and equitable access to public information for the public good.

Open DOT is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any government agency. All services are provided completely free of charge.
